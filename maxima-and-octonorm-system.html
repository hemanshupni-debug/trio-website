ABOUT SYSTEMS

What Are Maxima & Octonorm Systems?

Maxima and Octonorm systems are advanced modular exhibition structures widely used for trade shows, expos, conferences, pavilions and international exhibitions.

These systems provide flexibility, premium aesthetics, fast installation and cost-effective solutions for brands looking to create a strong exhibition presence.

MAXIMA SYSTEM

Premium Exhibition Structure Solution

Maxima systems offer superior aesthetics, stronger structural profiles and premium finishes, making them ideal for corporate brands.

  • Premium Appearance
  • Reusable Structure
  • Corporate Finish
  • Strong Framework
OCTONORM SYSTEM

Cost Effective & Flexible Solution

Octonorm systems are modular, lightweight and highly adaptable exhibition booth solutions suitable for all event scales.

  • Affordable Solution
  • Fast Installation
  • Easy Transportation
  • Flexible Layouts

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Maxima and Octonorm systems?

Maxima systems offer premium structural profiles and a modern appearance, while Octonorm systems are lightweight, modular, and cost-effective for exhibitions and trade shows.

Are Maxima and Octonorm stands reusable?

Yes, both Maxima and Octonorm systems are built as reusable modular structures, making them a cost-effective choice for brands that exhibit regularly.

Do you provide installation services?

Yes, Trio Exhibitions provides complete booth installation, dismantling, logistics, and on-site support services.

Can you execute exhibition projects internationally?

Yes, we deliver exhibition booth solutions across India, Dubai, Germany, Thailand, and various international exhibition venues.

Can Maxima and Octonorm booths be customized?

Absolutely, we provide custom branding, graphics, lighting integration, meeting areas, product displays, and complete stand customization.

What industries use Maxima and Octonorm exhibition stands?

Maxima and Octonorm systems are widely used in healthcare, technology, manufacturing, food, automotive, education and industrial exhibitions worldwide.

How long does it take to build a Maxima or Octonorm booth?

The timeline depends on booth size and complexity, but most modular stands can be installed much faster than traditional custom-built exhibition booths.

Do you provide 3D booth design before fabrication?

Yes, we create detailed 3D exhibition stand designs and visual presentations for client approval before production begins.

Are Maxima and Octonorm systems suitable for large exhibitions?

Yes, both systems are ideal for small, medium and large-scale exhibitions, trade fairs, expos and international events.

What are the advantages of modular exhibition stands?
