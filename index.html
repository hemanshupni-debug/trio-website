Trio Exhibitions is a leading exhibition stall design and booth fabrication company serving China, India, Dubai, Thailand, and Germany. With over 18 years of experience, we specialize in China-focused exhibition stand design, 3D space planning, and end-to-end turnkey solutions for trade shows, exhibitions, and corporate events. 我们为中国客户提供专业的展览摊位设计与展台搭建服务，拥有18年以上行业经验。

Our in-house team of designers, engineers, and skilled craftsmen handles every stage of your exhibition journey — from initial concept and 3D visualization to fabrication, on-site installation, and dismantling. We work with premium materials including Octonorm and Maxima structures, and deliver both modular and fully custom-built exhibition stands for China trade shows and international pavilions.

We have successfully executed projects at major venues including Jio World Convention Centre Mumbai, and international pavilions such as the Korea Pavilion and Dubai Lab Expo. Whether you need a compact booth or a large-scale country pavilion for a China exhibition, Trio Exhibitions ensures your brand makes a lasting impression on the exhibition floor.

Looking for an experienced exhibition stall fabricator for your China trade show, or based in Mumbai, Delhi, or Bangalore? Get in touch with our team for a free consultation and quote.
