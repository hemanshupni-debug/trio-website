Frequently Asked Questions

What furniture is available for rent?

We offer sofas, chairs, tables, reception counters, bar stools, lounge furniture and more.

How much does exhibition furniture rental cost?

Pricing depends on furniture type, quantity, duration and event location.

Do you provide delivery?

Yes, we provide delivery across exhibition venues and event locations.

Do you provide setup?

Yes, our team handles installation, arrangement and dismantling.

Can I rent furniture for a single-day exhibition?

Yes, we provide flexible rental options for one-day events, exhibitions, conferences and trade shows.

Do you offer furniture rental across India?

Yes, we provide exhibition and event furniture rental services across major cities and exhibition venues in India.

Can I customize my furniture selection?

Absolutely. You can choose furniture based on your booth design, branding requirements and event theme.

Do you provide lounge furniture for VIP areas?

Yes, we offer premium lounge setups, sofas, coffee tables and VIP seating solutions for exhibitions and events.

Are reception counters available on rent?

Yes, we provide reception counters, information desks and branded welcome counters for exhibitions and trade fairs.

Can furniture be branded with our company logo?

Yes, selected furniture and counters can be customized with company branding and graphics.

How far in advance should I book exhibition furniture?

We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks before the event to ensure availability and smooth delivery.

Why choose Trio Exhibitions for furniture rental?

We provide premium-quality furniture, timely delivery, professional setup and reliable support for exhibitions and events.
